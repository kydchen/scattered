Scattered

Scattered is a minimalist, local-first canvas for freely arranging and connecting notes. It is designed for Pencil, touch, and mouse, without templates or a forced hierarchy.

Scattered 是一个极简、本地优先的自由思考画布，适配 Pencil、触控和键鼠。它没有模板或强制层级，让想法自由分布、按需连接。

Local first / 本地优先

The app works without an account. Boards are stored in the current browser and can be exported as JSON. On the recommended hosted version, Google Drive sync is optional and begins only after the user explicitly connects an account.

应用无需账户即可使用，画布保存在当前浏览器中，并可导出为 JSON。推荐的在线版本提供可选的 Google Drive 同步，只有用户主动授权后才会启用。
